AJR

Let me set the scene for you:

I had just returned home from a listening party for the new Glass Animals album, and I was playing Mario Kart on the couch with my sister. All of a sudden, I got the worst stomach ache I have ever experienced. EVER. It started off just a normal amount of painful, but as a couple hours went by, it didn't stop and only got worse. The pain was so intense. Before I knew it, I was throwing up (ew, I know, what does this have to do with AJR? Hang in there.), and I had begun to think it was food poisoning, but the pain did not cease. I told my mom we needed to go to the ER, because I literally felt like I was going to die.

We went to the ER, I did a CAT scan, and sure enough, my appendix was about to burst. I had to unexpectedly go into surgery to get it removed, but I am so glad I caught it before it had burst. I stayed that night in the hospital and returned home the next day to recover.

Now, here is where AJR comes in: My sister and I had AJR tickets for two days after I had my appendectomy. I was determined to go, because I did not have ticket insurance and we had paid a lot of money for these tickets. We went, and I was so grateful when I found that I had an aisle seat, which was no more than 50 steps from the door and restroom, in case I needed to leave or if I got nauseous. I had a wonderful concert experience and was still able to stand and sing, but I did not dance as much as I usually do at concerts, understandably. It was such good music and visuals, AJR are an amazing trio of performers and definitely put on a great show.

I got to redeem that experience when I saw them again this year - of course to my luck, it was a couple weeks after I had torn my meniscus. My sister and I joke that every time I book an AJR concert, it probably means I should get ticket insurance, because I tend to get sick or injured right before. 😀

A R I Z O N A

This concert was so amazing. I love ARIZONA's music - it is the perfect mix of indie rock with synths, creating a dreamy and romantic sound to their music. The cherry on top is the lead singer's voice is literally perfect - I always tell people his voice is like butter.

I have been listening to them since their first album, so this concert was nostalgic (pun intended) for me. It was sadly their last tour as a band, as they will be splitting up for the unforeseen future. I hope they get back together again one day, but for now, I am glad I was able to take the time to see them live for my first, and hopefully not last, time.

Fittingly, they called this their Victory Lap tour.

BETWEEN FRIENDS

This one was a HOOT. I went with my sister, as well as one of my old missionary companions and her friend. Between Friends just released their new album WOW! - a dance album that was a very stark change from their previous work. I am a huge fan of anything dance music, so I have been looking forward to this album since the first song was released.

We were in a small venue, and you could see the stage well from anywhere within there. During their song JAM!, they had planned to play an extended version of it, repeating the song twice. After it was over, the sound mix had glitched, and they ended up playing it for us a THIRD TIME. It was soooo much fun and we laughed so hard, and of course we sang the lyrics louder and louder with each play through.

My old missionary companion and I agreed this one was what we needed at this busy time of being seniors in college - we just needed to sing and dance without a care in the world, and remember that life really is simple and can be full of singing, dancing, and letting your hair down if you let it be that way! (Cheesy, I know.)

BUNT.

My sister asked me one day if I wanted to go see BUNT. at the local venue. When she had asked me it was only about two or three days before the show, so it was very unplanned. But we rarely attend concerts here in town, and I heard a few songs he had done with other EDM artists I liked, so I was said why not?

BUNT. is from Germany and is one of the lead artists in a subgenre of dance called "stutter house". If you hear a song of his, you'll know exactly what I mean. His show was so high energy, and he played a great set of music, mixing a blend of his own songs with other dance songs, some being very popular and some being really niche ones that I hadn't heard in years! I had a blast and was so glad we got tickets on a whim.

This was the first show we had been to in a long time after a while, so this was the concert that reminded me how much I love live music.

Glass Animals

Boy do I love me some Glass Animals. This concert was space themed, alongside their new album (yes, the album I went to a listening party for before my appendix decided it didn't want to hang out with me anymore). The stage was set up like it was the control room of a spaceship, and the visuals and lights all had a space feel to them too. They played a lot of songs from their new album, but I do wish they had played more from their previous album 'Dreamland', which is my favorite one of theirs.

The set was short but so good. My sister and I were among the few people dancing in our section, as most of them were just sitting down and being lame in my opinion haha. I didn't care though - we were there to enjoy the music! Glass Animals only releases an album every four to five years, so we enjoyed this concert to its fullest.

half*alive

My sister and I first saw half alive live opening for Twenty One Pilots in Denver. I had never listened to them before, but I was immediately hooked! Their music is so unique and so fun, and very uplifting when it comes to the lyrics. We knew we had to catch them live when they came to town. They incorporate a lot of dancing into their live performances, and even had a dance break during the show while the band reset for the last half of the set. The lead singer came and crowd surfed over us, and it was so cool to help get him to surf back onto stage! He almost made it, then landed right next to me and got his mic pack stuck on someone's belt next to me. He was late to stage because of it, but made it right in time. I always recommend half alive as a good concert to go with friends and even family.

LIGHTS

I have been listening to LIGHTS since I was about 11, and this was my first time seeing her live. She played songs that I had on repeat in junior high and her new stuff, and I definitely shed a few happy tears. The room was so hot though - I definitely do not recommend the venue we had attended. They did not open any doors or have any ventilation, so it got so hot that some people had to leave to step outside mid concert. Even LIGHTS herself said we won for the warmest concert. I still had a blast. Her sound has been part of what has shaped my taste in music, and she is changing a lot as an artist, so I am interested to see what the future holds for her music.

Madeon

This concert was SO much fun. My sister and I drove outta town to catch Madeon and had so much fun.

Madeon's new album Victory is incredible. The visuals for the concert were so cool and just threw you right into the world that the music was built off of. My sister and I grew up on Madeon's music through our teenage years, so it was surreal to see him perform live.

Honestly I only had one complaint. The venue he played at was so small, and the people were not dancing or singing or cheering. So it was a bit weird considering this guy played at Coachella and plays huge arenas and stadiums all the time. Even though the people were lame, my sister and I still danced our feet off!

+ Martin Garrix +

This concert was NOT planned! I went to this one at a festival in FINLAND. I was on the study abroad trip this summer (greatest trip of all time, and I hope every CS and cyber student goes when they can) and one day we were in the subway trying to get back to our hotel when we saw the posters for this festival on the wall. I saw Martin Garrix was the headliner, so we all decided to buy tickets immediately, just the day before!

I am so glad I went. Europe is the home of dance music, so I knew I needed to go to an EDM festival while in Europe. I also grew up on some of his music, so it was so cool to hear some of the old hits. His visuals for the show were unreal, and these pictures do not do it full justice. He had lasers and bright lights and it was really well done for how big the crowd and festival was.

This concert was a core memory within a core memory!

Owl City

Now, you may think I have been listening to LIGHTS for a long time, but believe it or not, I have been listening to Owl City for longer. I first heard him on the bus in the 4th grade, and I have been listening since. He came to town for his Cinematic tour, and I begged my mom to take me. She took me and my sister, and it was so surreal to see Owl City live. Though his music can be super weird, it was what I grew up on, and I loved hearing those songs live. My little sister was so young and got so tired that she sat on the floor the whole time (LOL). I loved this concert and sadly do not have many pictures, but I won't forget it!

Porter Robinson

This concert was one for the books. My sister and I went and saw Porter Robinson and it was by far the most fun she and I have ever had at a concert. It was one of the most packed rooms I have ever been in, and though we were getting shoved around like crazy, we still had a great time. This concert was also the longest one I have ever been to, at a staggering 3 hours. We didn't get home until after 1 am that night. Porter played all of his albums - we called it the Porter Robinson "Era's Tour" - and did each album one at a time, rather than all mixed together. He would change the visuals and stage setup for each album theme, the first one being a lot like a rock concert, the next portion a very acoustic, laid back concert, and then the final part of the set being like a DJ set with dance music. My back killed me at the end from standing for nearly 6 hours but this concert took the cake!

Shawn Mendes

I am not a Shawn Mendes fan, but I was very lucky to go see him, as my friend invited me with her for her birthday. Her parents got her tickets, and he is her favorite artist ever. Shawn had a giant rose on the B stage, and I thought the circle-shaped screen was interesting. Everyone got light up bracelets that night, and they would change color and light up with each song. I remember Shawn singing Fix You by Coldplay, and all the bracelets sparkled blue and it made the whole arena look like a sky of stars. I love Coldplay, so it is kind of funny that one was my favorite song of the night, and not even one Shawn Mendes wrote.

Twenty One Pilots

I love this band so much. I have been listening to them since I was about 14 years old, and my first concert of theirs was when I was in 9th grade. We traveled outta town to go with some family friends. It was my first real concert, and Jon Bellion opened for them, who is another artist I really like. I remember being surprised when he came out on stage to open and I had no idea he would have been there. Then, Twenty One Pilots came out, and that was the first time I was ever truly starstruck. The energy in the room was so high and I sang and danced the whole time.

Ever since that concert, I have tried to catch every tour when I can. In 2021, my sister and I flew to Denver, CO where I went and waited in line for 12 hours for a good spot in the pit. It was their takeover tour, where they started in a small venue in the city and then worked up through the week to the arena venue. We went to the second smallest venue, and it was such a small and intimate show. It was a unique experience that only that tour was able to offer, as they will be selling out arenas for the rest of the tours to come.

This upcoming tour isn't coming anywhere near us, so I am sad to be missing it. It will be their first tour in 4 album cycles that we will be missing. But I will attend from afar and hope that the next tour and album aren't too far away.
